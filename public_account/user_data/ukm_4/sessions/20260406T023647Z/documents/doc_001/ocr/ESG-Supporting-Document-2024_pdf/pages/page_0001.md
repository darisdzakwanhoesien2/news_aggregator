## 1.3 Materiality Analysis

### Materiality Identification

The definition of the topics addressed in this report was conducted through a process of double materiality: combining impact materiality proposed by the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) standard and financial materiality from the Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB) standard. Impact materiality was defined in 2022 with the aim of determining the most relevant environmental, social, and governance (ESG) topics for management and using them as input for strategy updates.
