This thesis investigates the use of large language models (LLMs) and neurosymbolic reasoning techniques to detect greenwashing in corporate sustainability disclosures.

The research focuses on:

• analyzing ESG-related corporate reports
• identifying sustainability claims and promises
• distinguishing between performative rhetoric and actionable sustainability commitments
• grounding claims with textual evidence
• reducing hallucination using structured reasoning strategies such as Chain-of-Thought and Self-Consistency
• improving transparency in ESG evaluation systems

The proposed system includes a pipeline consisting of:

document ingestion → OCR → text normalization → prompt strategies → reasoning trace parsing → claim extraction → evidence grounding → greenwashing classification → evaluation.
