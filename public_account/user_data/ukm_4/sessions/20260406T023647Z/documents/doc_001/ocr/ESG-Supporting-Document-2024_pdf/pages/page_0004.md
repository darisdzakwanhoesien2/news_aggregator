# Material Issues for External Stakeholders

1. Industry perception: Proactively identify socio-environmental issues affecting communities and resolve them. For example the negative impacts of air emissions on the environment and community.

MASISA Chile has fixed industrial sources at its facilities, which have high-tech abatement systems installed, including an electrostatic precipitator, bag filters and gas scrubbers. This allows maintaining low emissions of suspended particles, as well as complying with current regulations. Reducing emissions of particulate matter has a direct impact on our environment, as mentioned in the air decontamination plans for the urban centers where the Masisa plants are located, and on the health of our local communities, as there is numerous evidence that particle pollution exposes people to premature death, lung and heart diseases and nonfatal heart attacks, as well as asthma.

Metrics: For the management of particulate matter in line with national regulations (D.S. N° 12/2021 - MMA), MASISA's environmental division has monitoring towers that provide information on respirable particulate matter (PM-10) as well as daily gas concentrations.
