# Supplier assessment and development

In line with our Corporate Procedure for Supplier Onboarding, Assessment, and Management, any company qualifying as a critical supplier seeking to provide goods and/or services to MASISA must complete an annual Supplier Assessment Survey, which is requested by MASISA's Supply team. The Supply, Wood Solutions and Forestry, Quality, SMS, and Community Relations departments are responsible for conducting the evaluation and/or reevaluation based on qualitative and quantitative information, considering general and specific criteria and parameters according to the type of supplier. Once the supplier completes this survey, MASISA evaluates it.

If a critical supplier receives a low rating during evaluation or reevaluation and, for strategic reasons, is irreplaceable, an Improvement Plan will be established with the supplier to address the negative aspects identified in the evaluation or reevaluation, along with a commitment to follow-up to monitor their progress.
