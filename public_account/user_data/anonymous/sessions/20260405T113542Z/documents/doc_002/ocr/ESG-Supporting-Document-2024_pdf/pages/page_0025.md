As part of MASISA's agreements regarding biodiversity, we monitor the aquatic biota of the Coihueco Stream semiannually (see report cover below). This stream is located within the area of influence of the Cabrero Plant. These monitoring activities help identify the effects of the plant's discharges on the stream, and no negative impacts have been observed on microalgae, fish, and macroinvertebrates.
